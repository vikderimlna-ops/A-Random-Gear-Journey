# Bosses

This page lists all major bosses in **A Random Gear Journey**.

⚠️ This is an unofficial, fan-made wiki.

---

## 🩸 Main Bosses

### 🪚 Sawpoint / Sawyer
**Role:** Psycho Berserker  
**Race:** Half Demon / Half Robot  
**Weapon:** Chainsaw Arm (Spawn Point Core)

**Description:**  
A traumatized, unstable fighter whose arm has been replaced with a chainsaw shaped like a Roblox spawn point. She has two spawn-point horns and a mind twisted by pain.

**Abilities & Mechanics:**  
• Blocks **ranged attacks** with her chainsaw arm  
• Enters an **Enraged Form** where she becomes nearly unstoppable  
• Sends **circular saws from the ground**  
• Can **predict player movement**  
• You must stay on **high platforms** to avoid ground saws  

⚠️ *Do NOT pick ranged characters against her.*

---

### 🧰 Eek11wa
**Role:** Glitched Builder / Summoner  
**Origin:** Escaped from Roblox Studio  
**Weapon:** Toolbox

**Description:**  
A broken test rig that gained consciousness and escaped the Studio. He’s the son of **Mother Rig** and still carries the power of development tools.

**Abilities & Mechanics:**  
• Builds **walls and structures**  
• Creates **powerful gears** for himself  
• Uses toolbox-based attacks  
• Alters the battlefield constantly  

---

### 💉 Mr. Cyro (The Doctor)
**Role:** Control / Debuffer  
**Weapon:** Needles

**Description:**  
Once a magician, Mr. Cyro lost his wife in a tragic accident. His grief turned him into “The Doctor.” He adopted Sawpoint and Eek11wa and is the reason Sawpoint survived and became powerful.

**Abilities & Mechanics:**  
• Throws and fires **poisonous needles**  
• Applies **slow and damage-over-time effects**  
• Uses tricks inspired by his magician past  
• Controls space and movement  

⚠️ *Avoid his needles at all costs.*

## 🚪 Behind the Gate

> These bosses appear after passing through the World Gate.

---

### 🟢 Verde
**Role:** Ranged DPS / Vengeance  
**Weapon:** Dual Green Hyperlaser Guns

**Description:**  
The oldest brother of Azul and Rojo. Left behind the Gate, abandoned by his siblings. His heart now burns with revenge.

**Battle Rules:**  
• You **cannot switch teammates** in this fight  
• You can only play as **Azul or Rojo**

**Abilities & Mechanics:**  
• Fires **extremely powerful lasers**  
• High damage and precision  
• Aggressive, relentless attacks  

---

### ⚕️ Corin
**Role:** Healer / Support Boss  
**Weapon:** Regeneration Coil

**Description:**  
Verde’s loyal helper. Corin saved him after Azul and Rojo left him behind and now supports him in every battle.

**Abilities & Mechanics:**  
• Heals **himself and Verde**  
• Constantly supports Verde  
• Close-ranged fighter  
• Must be defeated quickly or Verde becomes overwhelming  

---

### 🧸 Cultist
**Role:** Summoner / Dark Mage  
**Mask:** Cute Bear Mask  
**Age:** Over 300 Years

**Description:**  
She looks like a child, but she’s ancient. Hides her face behind a bear mask and summons demons to do her bidding.

**Abilities & Mechanics:**  
• Summons **demons with Bluesteel Claws**  
• Controls the battlefield with minions  
• Deceptively cute, brutally deadly  

---

### 👁️ Spectator (Final Boss)
**Role:** World Controller  
**Height:** 5km+ Tall  
**Eye:** Single Big Red Eye

**Description:**  
The final enemy. He collected every toolbox and gained control over reality itself. Watching everything… judging everything.

**Abilities & Mechanics:**  
• Uses **toolbox-based world manipulation**  
• Massive scale attacks  
• Controls terrain and gravity  
• Final test of everything you’ve learned  
• **Earthquake,** Slams the ground, creating massive shockwaves that knock players down and break platforms

---

*Only the strongest will survive the Spectator’s gaze…*
